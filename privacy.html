Privacy policy

Last updated · September 29, 2026

Verified Front Office LLC ("we", "us") provides front-office services to dental practices. This policy explains how we handle information.

This website

This website does not use forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type.

Patient information

We handle patient information only on behalf of dental practices, under a business associate agreement with each practice and as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) requires. Patients should direct questions about their records to their dental practice.

Text messages

We send text messages on behalf of the dental practices we serve, as described in our text messaging terms.

Mobile numbers and consent

We do not share or sell mobile phone numbers, or text messaging opt-in data and consent, with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, other than the service providers that deliver the messages for us.
